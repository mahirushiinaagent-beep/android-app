Speaker verification asks “who is speaking?” and is separate from speech recognition (“what was said?”). It is a probabilistic check with configurable thresholds, not a guarantee that only you can access ALISA: similar voices or good recordings can sometimes pass, and you can be wrongly rejected. Replay checks are heuristics. Sensitive commands need a fresh high-confidence neural match or your backup passcode; if verification is uncertain or unavailable the passcode is required, and a not-recognized voice is denied. Processing is on-device; recordings are never saved — only an encrypted voice reference and coarse fingerprints, erased by “Delete voice profile”. Browser storage is not hardware-backed secure storage: a compromised device, browser or extension could access it.
